What guides us

Make technology leave room for its user.

User agency

Systems should expose real choices, honest state and understandable controls instead of deciding on a person's behalf.

Open & ownable

Local control, interoperability and open standards let people keep using, adapting and repairing what they rely on.

Expressive authorship

Technology can be coherent without becoming anonymous. People should be able to make their environment feel like theirs.

What we’re building

One ecosystem.
Lots of weird little doors.

Each project solves a different part of the computing experience, but they share the same priorities: openness, interoperability, local control and room for personality.

Why we build

Serious systems.
Human fingerprints.

Made from the culture, not merely for it.

We build from lived communities, open-source practice and research rather than treating people as a market segment. The goal is technology that can be understood, adapted, repaired, played with and genuinely owned.

知は務め

Knowledge is a sacred duty.

Research should not disappear behind a product launch. We want the reasoning, experiments and trade-offs to remain visible.
