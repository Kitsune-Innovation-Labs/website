Why we build

Technology should leave room for its user.

Made from the culture, not merely for it.

We build from lived communities, open-source practice and research rather than treating people as a market segment. The aim is computing that can be understood, adapted, repaired, expressed through and genuinely owned.

Research made visible

Ideas should survive contact with implementation.

Kitsunebi is our clearest public example: research, design decisions and working components are developed side by side in the Lab.
