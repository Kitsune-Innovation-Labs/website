Two independent projects, one experience

The power of Awoo. The personality of Nyaa.

We want each component to stand on its own. Together, they would become UwU Desktop; separately, they should remain useful to people who prefer other parts of the Linux ecosystem.

⚡
The Wayland compositor · Planned

Awoo Compositor

The engine that manages windows, workspaces, rendering and input. Our goal is intuitive tiling that stays where you expect, flexible floating layouts and polished, configurable motion — without fighting the window manager.

Nyaa Shell

The face of the desktop: panels, launchers, notifications, widgets, themes and user-defined Spaces. Nyaa is intended to run on an existing compatible Wayland compositor while Awoo is still being developed.

The idea behind the name

Whimsical outside.
Serious engineering inside.

An approachable, playful identity shouldn't come at the cost of performance, reliability, accessibility or control. We want a desktop that welcomes newcomers and still gives enthusiasts room to tinker.

“Powered by Awoo, dressed by Nyaa, built on Arch.”— The proposed UwU Desktop family
Rethinking the desktop

Windows are tools. Your work is the point.

Today, most desktops organize applications and workspaces. We're exploring a different layer: persistent, named Spaces that help you keep the right context around the activity you choose — while preserving the familiar desktop for everyone who wants it.

▣

Spaces with purpose

Teaching, development, creativity or leisure — each Space could remember its pinned resources, layout preferences and visual atmosphere.

⌁

Fluid, not restless

Responsive motion and flexible tiling shouldn't mean unexpected scrolling or windows moving without a clear reason. You stay in charge.

✧

Yours, all the way down

Configurable panels, themes, workflows and accessible controls. No mandatory cloud, AI assistant or account to use your desktop.

Modularity by design

Use them together. Or don't.

Our proposed architecture separates the interface from the compositor. Nyaa can begin life on existing compositors; Awoo can eventually run other compatible desktop shells. The integration layer would use supported Wayland protocols and compositor-specific adapters where needed.

How we'd approach it

First Nyaa. Then Awoo.

Our current direction is deliberately incremental. These are intentions, not completed milestones or promised delivery dates.

01 · First prototypeNyaa on an existing compositor

Experiment with the interface, launcher and Spaces without needing to replace the whole Wayland session.

02 · Separate engineBuild Awoo with Smithay

Explore predictable tiling, floating windows, input handling and compositing in isolated test sessions.

03 · Long-term integrationBring UwU Desktop together

Test the combined experience, interoperability, accessibility and packaging before considering a release.

Project status: design and architecture exploration. There is no public release, download or release date to announce. Supporting other compositors and restoring application state are engineering goals whose compatibility must be tested.
