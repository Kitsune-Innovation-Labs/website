Long-term aspiration
Under the magic

Designed around your agency.

We envision Companions as a character and identity layer powered by adaptable, model-agnostic agent infrastructure — not a single required model, cloud subscription or persona.

01 / CHARACTER

Identity & expression

Appearance, personality, preferences and expressive behaviour shaped by the user.

02 / CONTINUITY

Memory with boundaries

Portable, permissioned memories, with clear ways to review, withhold or forget information.

03 / CAPABILITY

Model-flexible intelligence

Use smaller local models where hardware allows, and optional stronger compute for bigger tasks. No single model defines the companion.

On-watch inference, reliable cross-device continuity and spatial AR are active design questions, not demonstrated capabilities. We'll need to validate performance, battery use, security and accessibility before making promises.
