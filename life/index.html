A different kind of wellbeing

Some days, rest is the adventure.

Health isn't a competition, and no two days ask the same things of us. We want Kitsune Life to make space for chronic conditions, disability, fluctuating energy and real life — without shame, broken streaks or pressure to do more.

When today is a little harder

The promise we want to make

Care without pressure.

Not a score that measures your worth. Not a set of rings to close. A supportive experience that respects your capacity, helps you notice patterns and leaves decisions in your hands.

Built with care

Your body isn't an algorithm.

We're exploring a wellbeing experience grounded in consent, accessibility and practical safeguards.

◌

Capacity-aware by design

Users define their own goals, limits and needs. Sensor signals don't override what you say about how you feel.

⌁

Private and in your control

We want local-first processing where practical, understandable permissions and clear control over sensitive information.

✚

Careful with health advice

Evidence-informed guidance and transparent uncertainty — never pretending an AI character is a clinician or a diagnostic tool.
